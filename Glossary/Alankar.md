# Alankar

**Alankar** (अलंकार, "ornament") is a term from Indian Classical Music for a melodic pattern built by running through the [[Sargam]] notes in a structured, repeating shape rather than a plain straight scale. The goal is to embellish/enhance the beauty of the notes and to build finger fluency and ear familiarity with the scale at the same time.

Alankars are also called **paltas**. They typically start simple (adjacent note pairs) and get progressively longer/more interleaved, and are always practiced both ascending and in reverse (descending).

## Basic shapes (from [[Lesson 01 - Basics of Piano & Alankars]])
- Pairs: `Sa Re · Re Ga · Ga Ma · ...`
- Triplets: `Sa Re Ga · Re Ga Ma · Ga Ma Pa · ...`
- Five-note groups: `Sa Re Ga Ma Re · Re Ga Ma Pa Ga · ...`

See [[Fingering Notation]] for how fingering is marked for these patterns.
