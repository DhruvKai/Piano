# Western Note Names (C D E F G A B)

The other common way to name notes (alongside [[Sargam]]) is the **Western letter-name system**: the 7 white keys inside one [[Octave]] are named **C D E F G A B**, then the pattern repeats — the next white key after B is C again, one octave higher.

This is a different system from Western **solfège** (Do Re Mi Fa Sol La Ti Do): solfège names are *movable* (Do can be any note, depending on key), while letter names are *fixed* — C is always the same key.

## Relationship to Sargam
This course maps `Sa = C` as the beginner default, giving:

| Sargam | Sa | Re | Ga | Ma | Pa | Dha | Ni | Sa |
|--------|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Letter name | C | D | E | F | G | A | B | C |

## The black keys
C D E F G A B only names the 7 white keys — for the 5 black keys in between (sharps and flats), see [[Sharps & Flats]].
