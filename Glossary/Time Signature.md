# Time Signature

A **time signature** is the pair of numbers at the start of a piece of music, like **4/4** or **3/4**. It tells you how the beat is grouped:

- **Top number** = how many beats are in each **bar** (also called a *measure*).
- **Bottom number** = which note length counts as one beat. A **4** means the quarter note gets the beat, so 4/4 and 3/4 both count in ordinary quarter-note beats.

The first beat of every bar, **beat 1** (the *downbeat*), is the strongest. Where beat 1 comes back is what makes music feel like a march or like a waltz.

## The two you need first

| Time | Beats per bar | Accent pattern | Count it | Feel |
| ---- | ------------- | -------------- | -------- | ---- |
| **4/4** ("common time") | 4 | **STRONG** – weak – medium – weak | **1** 2 3 4 · **1** 2 3 4 | steady; most pop, rock and film songs |
| **3/4** | 3 | **STRONG** – weak – weak | **1** 2 3 · **1** 2 3 | swaying; the **waltz** feel |

## Counting faster notes
When a pattern has two notes per beat, count the in-between notes as **"&"** ("and"):

- 4/4: **1** & **2** & **3** & **4** & → 8 even notes per bar
- 3/4: **1** & **2** & **3** & → 6 even notes per bar

Say the numbers on the beats and "&" between them. It keeps the notes even and shows where beat 1 falls.

## Telling which one a song is in
Tap or clap along to the song and count. Then try putting "**1**" on the strongest beat:
- If **1 2 3 4 · 1 2 3 4** lines up with the strong beats, it's in 4/4.
- If **1 2 3 · 1 2 3** fits better and the song sways side to side, it's in 3/4.

## With a metronome
Most [[Metronome]] apps and keyboards have a **beats-per-bar** (or "time signature") setting that makes beat 1 click louder or in a different tone. Set it to **4** for 4/4 or **3** for 3/4, so you hear where each bar starts.
