# Sargam

**Sargam** is the Indian classical solfège system — the equivalent of Western Do-Re-Mi. It names the 7 notes of a scale:

| <span style="background-color:#2c6fbb;color:#fff;padding:1px 6px;border-radius:3px;">Sargam</span> | Sa | Re | Ga | Ma | Pa | Dha | Ni | Sa |
|--------|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| <span style="background-color:#c9622a;color:#fff;padding:1px 6px;border-radius:3px;">Western (movable-do)</span> | Do | Re | Mi | Fa | Sol | La | Ti | Do |

`Sa` and `Pa` are fixed reference notes (like the tonic and dominant); the others can shift slightly depending on the raga/scale being used. For beginner piano exercises, Sargam is used the same way a Western teacher would use scale degrees.
