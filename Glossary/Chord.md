# Chord (Triad)

A **chord** — specifically a **triad**, the simplest kind — is 3 notes played together: the [[Root Note]], its 3rd, and its 5th (counting up the [[Major Scale]] degrees from the root).

## Major vs. minor triads
Both are built the same way (root + 3rd + 5th), but the 3rd sits in a different spot:

| Triad | Root → 3rd | 3rd → 5th |
|---|---|---|
| Major | 4 half steps ([[Sharps & Flats\|semitones]]) | 3 half steps |
| Minor | 3 half steps | 4 half steps |

Example: **C major** = C – E – G (C→E is 4 semitones). **C minor** = C – Eb – G (C→Eb is only 3 semitones) — only the middle note moves.

## Fingering convention
This course plays triads as **RH: 1 3 5** (thumb-middle-pinky) and **LH: 5 3 1** (mirrored) — see [[Fingering Notation]].
