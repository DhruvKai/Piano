# Fingering Notation

How finger positions are marked in the [[PIX Series - Course Overview|PIX Series]] course. **Note this differs from standard classical piano fingering**, where the thumb is always `1` and fingers go `1`(thumb)–`5`(pinky).

| Label | Finger |
|-------|--------|
| `Th` | Thumb |
| `1` | Index |
| `2` | Middle |
| `3` | Ring |
| `4` | Pinky |

Example — fingering for the ascending base scale in [[Lesson 01 - Basics of Piano & Alankars]]:

| Note | Sa | Re | Ga | Ma | Pa | Dha | Ni | Sa |
|------|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Finger | 1 | 2 | Th | 1 | 2 | 3 | Th | 1 |

The `Th` marks are the classic **thumb-under** technique: the thumb tucks underneath the hand to keep the scale moving smoothly without resetting hand position.
