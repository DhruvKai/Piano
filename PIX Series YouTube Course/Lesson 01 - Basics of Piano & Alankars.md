## Overview
First lesson of the series — introduces [[Alankar|Alankars]] (ornamentation exercises from Indian Classical Music) built on the [[Sargam]] scale, plus the finger positions used to play them.

## Key Concepts
- [[Alankar]]s embellish/enhance the beauty of a musical phrase — built from the 7 [[Sargam]] notes: `Sa Re Ga Ma Pa Dha Ni Sa`
- The base ascending scale uses the thumb-under technique — see [[Fingering Notation]]

## The Base Scale & Fingering

| Note | Sa | Re | Ga | Ma | Pa | Dha | Ni | Sa |
|------|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|----|
| Finger | 1 | 2 | Th | 1 | 2 | 3 | Th | 1 |

(`Th` = thumb tucks under the hand — see [[Fingering Notation]] for the full labeling scheme.)

## Practice Patterns (Paltas)
All three patterns should also be practiced **in reverse**, using the same finger positions as the ascending scale above.

**Pattern 1 — pairs**
`Sa Re · Re Ga · Ga Ma · Ma Pa · Pa Dha · Dha Ni · Ni Sa`

**Pattern 2 — triplets**
`Sa Re Ga · Re Ga Ma · Ga Ma Pa · Ma Pa Dha · Pa Dha Ni · Dha Ni Sa`

**Pattern 3 — five-note groups**
`Sa · Sa Re Ga Ma Re · Re Ga Ma Pa Ga · Ga Ma Pa Dha Ma · Ma Pa Dha Ni Pa · Pa Dha Ni Sa`

## Exercises / Practice
- [ ] Base scale Sa→Sa with `Th` fingering, ascending and descending
- [ ] Pattern 1 (pairs) ascending + reverse
- [ ] Pattern 2 (triplets) ascending + reverse
- [ ] Pattern 3 (five-note groups) ascending + reverse
