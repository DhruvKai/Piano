# Lesson 02 — Western Notes & Right Hand Exercises

> [!info] Source
> 🎥 [Lesson #2: Learn Western Notes (C D E F G A B) & Right Hand Exercises](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vGqDwsaJjeI&list=PL-C4jgLVkaodZP587mztVMk0hd7vGwGVS&index=3) — **PIX Series**
> 📄 Companion PDF: [Right Hand Exercises](https://pixseries.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/03/Lesson-2-Right-Hand-Exercises.pdf) via [pixseries.com/lesson-2](https://pixseries.com/lesson-2/)

## Overview
Introduces the [[Western Note Names]] (C D E F G A B) and moves into right-hand-only exercises that walk stepwise through them, building finger independence within one [[Octave]].

## Key Concepts
- White keys are named C D E F G A B, repeating every [[Octave]] — see [[Western Note Names]] for how this lines up with [[Sargam]].
- These exercises stay within a 7-note span (C to B/C) using the right hand only.

## Right Hand Exercises
![[keyboard-one-octave.svg]]

Straight from the companion PDF. Each line is one pass — play left-to-right, and repeat until comfortable before moving to the next exercise.

**Exercise 1** — 8-note stepwise up-and-back, shifting up one note each line:
```
C D E F G F E D
D E F G A G F E
E F G A B A G F
F G A B C B A G
G A B C D C B A
A B C D E D C B
B C D E F E D C
C D E F G F E D
```

**Exercise 2** — a tighter zig-zag shape, shifting up one note each line:
```
C E D F E D C D
D F E G F E D E
E G F A G F E F
F A G B A G F G
G B A C B A G A
```

**Exercise 3** — a wider skip-and-step shape, shifting up one note each line:
```
C G F E D C D E
D A G F E D E F
E B A G F E F G
F C B A G F G A
```

> [!tip] Fingering
> The PDF doesn't mark specific finger numbers for these (unlike Lesson 01's Alankars). Apply the same [[Fingering Notation]] approach — thumb (`Th`) under on the wider jumps — and settle on whatever fingering keeps the hand relaxed.

## Exercises / Practice
> [!tip] Practice with a [[Metronome]]
> Play each exercise along with a slow, steady click rather than at your own pace — see [[Metronome]] for how to ramp up tempo safely.

- [ ] Exercise 1, all 8 lines, right hand only
- [ ] Exercise 2, all 5 lines, right hand only
- [ ] Exercise 3, all 4 lines, right hand only
- [ ] Say the letter names out loud while playing, to internalize [[Western Note Names]]
