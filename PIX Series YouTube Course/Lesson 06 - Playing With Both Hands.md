## Overview
Combines everything so far: the **left hand holds a chord** (see [[Chord]]) while the **right hand plays a moving line** (a [[Major Scale]] or a short pattern from it) on top. This is the core skill [[Arpeggio|arpeggios]] build on next.

## Key Concepts
- LH = held chord (root position), RH = the moving part — two independent things happening at once.
- The source PDF only writes out 5 keys per exercise as a template, then says: *"note down all other scales by yourself in above given format and practice them at maximum BPM."* Treat the tables below as a starting template, not the full 12.

## Exercise 1 — LH holds root chord, RH plays the full scale up and down

| <span style="background-color:#2c6fbb;color:#fff;padding:1px 6px;border-radius:3px;">Key (LH)</span> | <span style="background-color:#c9622a;color:#fff;padding:1px 6px;border-radius:3px;">RH ascending</span> |
|---|---|
| C | C – D – E – F – G – A – B – C |
| G | G – A – B – C – D – E – F# – G |
| D | D – E – F# – G – A – B – C# – D |
| A | A – B – C# – D – E – F# – G# – A |
| E | E – F# – G# – A – B – C# – D# – E |

Then play RH in reverse for the way down (e.g. C: C–B–A–G–F–E–D–C) while LH keeps holding the same chord throughout.

## Exercise 2 — LH holds root chord, RH plays a shorter up-and-partly-back pattern

| <span style="background-color:#2c6fbb;color:#fff;padding:1px 6px;border-radius:3px;">Key (LH)</span> | <span style="background-color:#c9622a;color:#fff;padding:1px 6px;border-radius:3px;">RH pattern</span> |
|---|---|
| C | C – D – E – F – G – F – E – D |
| G | G – A – B – C – D – C – B – A |
| D | D – E – F# – G – A – G – F# – E |
| A | A – B – C# – D – E – D – C# – B |
| E | E – F# – G# – A – B – A# – G# – F# |

*Note: the E row's last three notes (`A# G# F#`) break the up-then-mirror pattern the other rows follow — transcribed as given in the source; worth double-checking against the video.*
